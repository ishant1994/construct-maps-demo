Objective

Identify which residues of the wasp proline-rich antimicrobial peptide Pdi1 drive its binding to the bacterial ribosome, separating binding determinants from determinants of uptake and potency.

Hypotheses

  • Which residues of the conserved C-terminal pharmacophore carry Pdi1's binding to the post-release ribosome, and how much does each contribute?
  • Does the free C-terminal carboxylate, as distinct from the Leu20 side chain, set how Pdi1 binds and whether it traps the release factor?
  • Is Pdi1 binding confined to its C-terminal segment (Api-like), or do its N-terminal cationic residues also contribute, as reported for drosocin?
  • Does the absence of an aromatic residue at the Pdi1 position equivalent to Api Tyr7 limit Pdi1's tunnel binding?
