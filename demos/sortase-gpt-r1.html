Sortase A ΔN59: P94 chemistry and β7–β8 loop coupling

Distinguish P94 side-chain chemistry, Y187 loop packing and functional coupling using eight soluble S. aureus ΔN59 constructs. All literature evidence was published before 2026-01-01.

164 aa

Hypothesis

Arg provides a distinct route to changing the P94-containing cleft wall.

Caveats: The evolved 5M benefit does not establish a single-P94R fold improvement in this tag-cleaved background. An LPETG-specific advantage could reflect substrate chemistry rather than loop opening.

Proposed edit

Substitute 1 residue in Sortase catalytic fold and Substrate-cleft wall / H1: P52R. Retains Purification handle, Tag spacer, TEV cleavage site, Cleavage scar, Calcium-coupled recognition loop and Nucleophile-access loop.

As described Introduces a positively charged side chain at the cleft wall.
