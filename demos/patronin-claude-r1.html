Hypothesis 1 Does the PrLD contribute to minus-end recognition at all, or does it only supply length and spacing between the other modules?

Rationale: If the PrLD merely separates the N-terminal CH region from the C-terminal coiled-coil, linker and CKK recognition module, an inert length-matched spacer should behave like the native domain. If any property of its sequence contributes, the spacer should resemble the deletion.
