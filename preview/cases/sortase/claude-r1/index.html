Objective

Find out whether P94 substitutions raise sortase A activity by releasing the closed β7-β8 loop (through Y187/W194 contact), through the chemistry of the introduced residue, or through a route independent of the loop, using one precisely edited ΔN59 core and matched controls.

Hypotheses

  • Is loss of the proline ring's packing against the closed β7-β8 loop enough to raise activity, whatever replaces it?
  • Does the charge of the introduced residue add to or subtract from the ring-removal effect?
  • Does the P94 gain depend on Y187 in the β7-β8 loop?
  • Is the P94 contribution still present once the other loop mutations of the evolved enzyme are in place?
