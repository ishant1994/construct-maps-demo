Objective

Decide whether the prion-like domain of Drosophila Patronin contributes to microtubule minus-end recognition through its specific primary sequence or through its capacity to condense, using evidence published before 2026-01-01.

Hypotheses

  • Does the PrLD contribute to minus-end recognition at all, or does it only supply length and spacing between the other modules?
  • Can an unrelated condensing disordered domain replace the PrLD, so that capacity to condense, and not the native sequence, is what is required?
  • Does recognition depend on the linear order of the native PrLD, or only on its composition?
  • Is condensation by the PrLD aromatic stickers necessary for its function?
  • Does fixed multivalency without liquid-like condensation substitute for the PrLD?
