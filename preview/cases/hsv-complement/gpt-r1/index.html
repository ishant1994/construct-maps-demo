Does complement engagement contribute to protection by antibodies against HSV-1 in neonatal mice, and does it do so across antibodies?

Objective

Test whether C1q-dependent complement engagement contributes to protection in neonatal mice and whether its contribution differs across antibody identities within and between gD and gB. Evidence cutoff: before 2026-01-01; preprints are explicitly labelled.

Hypotheses

  • Does C1q recruitment contribute to protection by HSV8?
  • Can stronger surface Fc assembly improve complement-dependent protection by HSV8?
  • Does C1q recruitment contribute to protection by CH42?
  • Does C1q recruitment contribute to protection by D48?
  • Can stronger surface Fc assembly improve complement-dependent protection by D48?
  • Does C1q recruitment contribute to protection by BMPC23?
