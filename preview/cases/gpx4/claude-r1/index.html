How can a designed binder to human GPX4 be turned into a degrader that removes GPX4 rather than only binding it?

Objective

Convert binder5, a BindCraft-designed binder to human GPX4 (P36969), from a molecule that only binds into a degrader that lowers GPX4 protein in cells. Four mechanistically distinct recruitment routes are compared, each against a matched dead or ablated control and the binder-only reference.

Hypotheses

  • Does a folded catalytic U-box E3 module on the binder turn GPX4 engagement into GPX4 ubiquitination and loss?
  • Does recruiting a CUL3 adapter (SPOP BTB/3-box) degrade GPX4, and does that need the 3-box?
  • Does a four-residue C-end degron suffice to degrade GPX4 through the binder?
  • Can a KFERQ-type motif route GPX4 to the lysosome without ubiquitination?
