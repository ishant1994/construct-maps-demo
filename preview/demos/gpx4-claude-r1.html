GPX4 binder5 → degrader: recruitment routes

Convert binder5, a BindCraft-designed binder to human GPX4 (P36969), from a molecule that only binds into a degrader that lowers GPX4 protein in cells. Four mechanistically distinct recruitment routes are compared, each against a matched dead or ablated control and the binder-only reference.

Binder + linker, no effectorBinder with linker only

82 aa

Hypothesis

Establishes what GPX4 engagement by the binder alone does to GPX4 level, GPX4 activity and the apparent immunoblot signal, and serves as the free competitor in an excess-binder experiment.

Caveats: Affinity, epitope and intracellular engagement of the binder are unmeasured; the binder carries no aromatic residues, which is the composition of an idealised helical-bundle core rather than a designed hydrophobic paratope, so a flat result in every arm would first point to non-engagement. Whether binding inhibits GPX4 is unknown, and GPX4 loss causes ferroptosis, so cells need ferrostatin-1 or liproxstatin-1 during sampling.
