S. aureus sortase A ΔN59: why P94 substitutions raise activity and whether the β7-β8 loop is involved

Find out whether P94 substitutions raise sortase A activity by releasing the closed β7-β8 loop (through Y187/W194 contact), through the chemistry of the introduced residue, or through a route independent of the loop, using one precisely edited ΔN59 core and matched controls.

P94AAlanine at the helix H1 start

147 aa

Hypothesis

Tests whether removing the proline ring, with no new charge or polarity, is enough to raise activity.

Caveats: Ala keeps Cβ within contact distance of Y187, and loss of the proline's backbone constraint at the first helical position may change helix capping independently of the loop. A wild-type-like result would not exclude a loop contribution.

Proposed edit

Substitute 1 residue in Helix H1 N-terminal turn: P35A. Retains Ca2+ site (beta3-beta4 loop), Beta4/H2 loop (catalytic His side), Beta6-beta7 loop and Beta7-beta8 loop.

As described Removes the ring that packs against Y187 and W194 in closed-loop structures and leaves a small neutral residue.

Hypothesis 1 Is loss of the proline ring's packing against the closed β7-β8 loop enough to raise activity, whatever replaces it?

Rationale: In apo and closed-loop structures the P94 ring is 3-4 Å from Y187 and W194; in the substrate-attached NMR complex it is more than 10 Å from both. Alanine removes the ring's contact surface and adds no charge or polarity, so a gain from P94A would point to released packing as sufficient.
